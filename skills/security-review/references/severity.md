# Severity

Assign Critical, High, Medium, or Low from demonstrated exploitability and impact in this **fixed point**, not from how alarming the weakness category sounds. Prefer the lowest band the evidence supports.

## Critical

Credible systemic compromise: broad authentication bypass, remote code execution, or widespread privileged compromise.

## High

Privilege escalation, cross-tenant access, meaningful sensitive-data disclosure, or a significant unauthorized operation.

## Medium

A real weakness whose exploitation is constrained (preconditions, blast radius, or required vantage) or whose security impact is limited.

## Low

A concrete security weakness with limited direct impact.

## Band choice

A published `SR-*` is a security issue with an established attack path. Style, naming, and other code-quality notes stay out of the report; they are not Low and they are not informational findings.
